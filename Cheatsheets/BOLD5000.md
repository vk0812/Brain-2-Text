# BOLD_5000

Large-scale, slow event-related fMRI dataset.

Collected on 4 subjects, each observing 5,254 images over 15 scanning sessions.

$$
\text{Image presentation } \to \text{fMRI acquisition } \to \text{GLM beta estimates } \to \text{Brain response vector}
$$

## Images

Images were presented for 1 second, with 9 seconds of fixation between trials. 
Participants were asked to judge whether they **liked, disliked, or were neutral** about the image.

| Source   | Number of images | Content                                    |
|----------|----------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| SUN      | 1000                 | Indoor and outdoor scenes                  |
| COCO     | 2000                 | Every day objects, people and interactions |
| ImageNet | 1916                 | Objects and objects categories             |
| Total    | 4916 (Some repeated) |                                            |

In total, the dataset contains 5254 stimulus representation, some images were repeated.

## Release 1.0

A participant looks at an image, different parts of the brain respond to its features.

**MRI scanner** measures changes in the magnetic resonance signal associated with blood oxygenation.

The **BOLD signal** is delayed.

### Slow event-related design

```plaintext
Time       0s                     1s                    10s
           |----------------------|----------------------|
Stimulus   [         IMAGE       ][   fixation cross     ]
           |                                              
           +---- brain response develops over time ----+
```

1. The patient looks at an image for $1s$.
2. It then fixes a blank screen for $10s$, to leave the brain time to respond.
